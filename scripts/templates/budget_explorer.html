About these figures

  • Adopted budget is what Borough Council planned for the year, from that year's adopted budget. It is a plan, not what was actually spent or collected.
  • Estimated actual is the borough's estimate, made late in the year, of what was actually spent or collected. It is printed in the following year's budget, so the latest available is for __LAST_ESTIMATE_YEAR__.
  • Figures are in nominal dollars: they are not adjusted for inflation.
  • These are the summary lines of each budget ("Revenue/Expenditures - all operating funds"), which cover the General, Sewer & Solid Waste, and Liquid Fuels funds.
  • Where a line was renamed between years it is shown as one line; the note under its name says so.
  • The 2023-2025 budgets are scanned images and were read with OCR software. Every total in them was checked against the sum of its rows, and misread figures were corrected by hand; those are marked †.
  • Each budget repeats the previous year's figures. Where it gives a different figure than the year's own budget did (usually a revision), the table marks it ‡; differences of a few dollars, from rounding, are not marked. A few printed totals in the borough's documents do not add up; those are marked §.
